# The name Shamaran

Shamaran takes its name from **Şahmaran**, the legendary human-and-serpent figure
preserved in Kurdish oral tradition and visual culture. The figure carries themes
of knowledge, healing, protection, nature, and the consequences of betrayal—ideas
that fit a project designed to remember, help, and act within clear boundaries.

Şahmaran has an especially strong place in Kurdish cultural memory and in the
material culture of Kurdish-speaking communities. Academic work describes the
figure as a Kurdish snake goddess and documents Şahmaran images in textiles, rugs,
jewelry, and homes. The story is also told in neighboring Mesopotamian and
Anatolian communities, with local variations among Kurdish, Arab, Syriac, Turkish,
and other peoples. Shamaran acknowledges that shared regional history while being
explicit about the project's Kurdish inspiration.

The software uses the spelling **Shamaran** for an internationally readable project
name. This cultural reference is a statement of inspiration, not a claim that one
software project owns the legend.

## Cultural care

Contributions should treat the name, symbol, and cultural references with respect.
Avoid flattening Şahmaran into an exotic decoration or presenting a single modern
retelling as the only authentic version.
